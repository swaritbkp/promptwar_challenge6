The Science Behind HawaKhata

← Back to Passbook

Core Principle: "Gemini Proposes, Code Decides"

LLMs understand natural language (EN/HI) but cannot reliably perform physical arithmetic. HawaKhata strictly separates responsibilities:

  • Deterministic Engine: Pure mathematical functions in Python calculate every dose, budget, purifier sizing, and emission delta.
  • Numeric Grounding Gate: Every LLM response is programmatically checked against tool outputs before display. Ungrounded numbers are rejected.
  • Deterministic Fallback: If Gemini is unavailable, rule-based templates answer immediately with verified numbers.

1. Daily Air Budget (WHO 2021)

WHO 2021 Global Air Quality Guidelines set 24-h PM2.5 threshold at 15 µg/m³:

Budget (µg/day) = 15 µg/m³ × Inhalation Volume (m³/day)

Inhalation rates follow US EPA Exposure Factors: children 6.0 m³/day, adults 12.0–16.0 m³/day, seniors 10.0 m³/day.

2. Cigarette Equivalence (Berkeley Earth)

Berkeley Earth rule-of-thumb translates ambient PM2.5 into tangible cigarette equivalents:

1 Cigarette/Day ≈ 22 µg/m³ PM2.5 inhaled over 24 hours

In severe smog (350 µg/m³ PM2.5), 24 hours of unfiltered outdoor air equals smoking ~16 cigarettes.

3. Indoor Mass-Balance Physics

Room air simulation solves the single-zone mass-balance differential equation:

V · (dC/dt) = p · a · V · C_out - (a · V + CADR + k_dep · V) · C_in + S_indoor

Exact exponential stepping guarantees mass conservation and non-negative concentration.

4. Privacy & Data Architecture

Zero personal health records or passwords on servers. Profiles stay in local storage; coordinates sent to Google Maps AQ API are rounded to ~1 km.
