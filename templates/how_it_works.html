The Science Behind HawaKhata

← Back to Passbook

Core Principle: "Gemini Proposes, Code Decides"

LLMs understand natural language (EN/HI) but physical arithmetic requires exact computation:

  • Deterministic Engine: Pure Python functions compute every dose, budget, and emission delta.
  • Numeric Grounding Gate: Checks LLM numbers against tool outputs; ungrounded numbers are rejected.
  • Deterministic Fallback: If Gemini is unavailable, rule-based templates answer with verified figures.

1. Daily Air Budget (WHO 2021)

WHO 2021 guidelines set the 24-h PM2.5 threshold at 15 µg/m³:

Budget (µg/day) = 15 µg/m³ × Inhalation Volume (m³/day)

EPA Inhalation Volumes (Table 6-1): Child (6–11y) 12.0 m³/day (Budget: 180 µg/d), Teen (11–16y) 15.2 m³/day (Budget: 228 µg/d), Adult (31–41y) 16.0 m³/day (Budget: 240 µg/d), Senior (61–71y) 14.2 m³/day (Budget: 213 µg/d).

2. Cigarette Equivalence (Berkeley Earth)

Berkeley Earth metric translates PM2.5 into cigarette equivalents:

1 Cigarette/Day ≈ 22 µg/m³ PM2.5 inhaled over 24 hours

3. Indoor Mass-Balance Physics

Room air simulation solves single-zone mass balance:

V·(dC/dt) = p·a·V·C_out - (a·V + CADR + k_dep·V)·C_in + S_indoor

Exact exponential stepping guarantees mass conservation and non-negative concentration.

4. Boundary-Layer Dispersion Physics

Open-Meteo 96-h planetary boundary layer height (BLH) and wind velocity determine winter dispersion:

  • Inversion Trap (BLH ≤ 350m): Shallow nocturnal inversion traps emissions at street level.
  • Afternoon Mixing Lift (BLH ≥ 800m, wind ≥ 8 km/h): Solar convection expands vertical dilution volume.
  • Wind Clearing (≥ 18 km/h) & Rain Washout (≥ 1mm): Advection flushing and atmospheric wet scavenging.

5. Privacy & Data Architecture

Zero personal health records on servers. Profiles stay in local storage; coordinates are rounded to ~1 km.

Governance, Scalability & Zero-Hallucination Design

4 pillars
1

"Gemini Proposes, Code Decides"

A deterministic numeric grounding gate rejects any figure that no engine tool produced, and EN/HI guardrails refuse medical-dosing and prompt-injection requests before the model is called.

2

Geo-cell caching & rate limiting

Forecasts are cached for 30 minutes per ~1 km rounded geo-cell (in-memory, or Redis when configured), and a per-client token-bucket limiter protects every /api/ route.

3

Circuit-broken 3-tier fallback

Open-Meteo → Google Air Quality → validated fixture. Each live provider sits behind a circuit breaker, so outages degrade gracefully with zero crashes.

4

Strict CSP & lean delivery

A strict default-src 'self' Content-Security-Policy, GZip-compressed responses, cached static assets, and zero external trackers or CDNs.
